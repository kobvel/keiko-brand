02 / The experience

Personality earns its place.

Recognise it.

The swirl remains the logo. Its expressive version becomes the coach’s profile image and a small presence in the conversation header.

Feel understood.

The warmth comes from remembering your situation, accepting corrections and giving a useful answer.

Keep it composed.

The character stays still while you read. Expressions are sparse; it never looks disappointed in you for missing training.

03 / Expression

A small emotional range.

AttentiveThe everyday face.
ConsideredA more focused look.
WarmA rare, earned acknowledgement.

Static expression studies. These are not progress indicators or automatic reactions to workout scores. A small smile is allowed; emotional pressure isn’t.

What the research supports

Memory, context and specific advice are the strongest basis for this direction. The research does not establish a winning avatar for Keiko’s athletes. The healthcare study concerns humanlike cartoons in triage, not an expressive swirl.

What I would test

Show the same conversations with the mark and the character, rotating their order. Ask what Keiko is, what feels credible, and what feels irritating. Check recognition at profile size and comfort after repeated use.
